About

What is Cricket Dugout?

A Discord-based cricket management and card collection experience. Build a team, collect cards, manage your Playing XI, compete in matches and grow your club.

🏏

Build Your Squad

Manage your squad, Playing XI, substitutes and reserves.

🃏

Collect Cards

Discover player cards, inspect stats and grow your collection.

⚔️

Play Matches

Start different match formats and follow the scoreboard and match flow.

💰

Economy

Buy, sell and trade eligible player cards while managing your resources.

⬆️

Upgrade

Upgrade eligible players and use skills and progression systems.

🎁

Rewards

Claim daily rewards, boxes, packs, quests and other progression rewards.

Core Commands

These commands are derived from the current command guide supplied for Cricket Dugout. Keep the live bot and this website synchronized whenever a command changes.

ddebutCreate your Cricket Dugout account and begin your journey.
dhelpOpen the main help menu.
duse allBrowse the detailed command usage guide.
dprofileView your Cricket Dugout profile and account summary.
dsquadView your squad, XI, substitutes and reserves.
dxiDisplay your current Playing XI.
dcaptainAssign a captain from your team setup.
dvcAssign a vice-captain.
dplay 1/2/5/10/20/soStart a match in a selected format.
dmmSend a matchmaking ping.
dtm <overs> <team size>Create or join multiplayer team matches.
dcard <player>View detailed player-card information.
dfindSearch players from the card collection.
dtrade @userStart a player-card trade.
dbuy <player>Purchase an eligible player card.
dsell <player>Sell a selected player card for coins.
dupgrade <player>Upgrade an eligible player's rating or attributes.
dclaimClaim a free random player reward when ready.
ddailyCollect the daily login/streak reward.
dshopOpen the in-game shop.
dquestView daily or weekly quests and progress.
dpassView Monthly Pass progress and rewards.
dspassView Season Pass progress and rewards.
denergyView Energy used for Pass Skills.
dskillsBrowse the skill shop.
dskillManage owned/equipped skills.
dlbView global leaderboards.
dgsView a player-card global statistics leaderboard.
dresultsReview recently completed match results.
dsbView the live/completed scoreboard.
dcareerBegin the Career Mode journey.

Game Systems

Everything in one place

Match

Match formats include 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 overs and Super Over in the current command guide. Multiplayer team formats are also supported by the documented command system.

Squad

Manage team name, Playing XI, substitutes/reserves, captain, vice-captain and player positions.

Cards & Stats

Inspect OVR, role, country, rarity and performance information, plus card-specific leaderboards.

Trading & Market

Use the market and secure trade flow to buy, sell and exchange eligible cards.

Progression

Level up, complete quests, claim rewards and progress through pass systems.

Career Mode

Create and customize a Career Mode player and progress from Gully Cricket toward the international stage.
